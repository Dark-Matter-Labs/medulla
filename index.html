members

We believe change needs to happen in tangible places: in rooms, courtyards, buildings, and neighborhoods where people can gather, build trust, and work together. Situated in a quiet Kreuzberg courtyard, Dresdener 113B serves as both a living room and a working space for developing the relationships, ideas, and infrastructures needed for resilient collective futures.

The ground floor hosts Medulla's public programming, including salons, lectures, workshops, gatherings, and other formats designed to build resilience, prosperity, and new civic imagination. The floor above is dedicated to focused work, hosting member organizations and individuals whose work contributes to Medulla's shared mission in distinct but mutually reinforcing ways.

Medulla is a physical space for turning shared urgency into collective resilience by building new collaborations and infrastructures of prosperity.

Medulla takes its name from Medulla Oblongata, a part of the human brain and a critical command center for involuntary, life-sustaining functions such as breathing, heart rate, and blood pressure. Taken as inspiration, Medulla is a response to the growing and compounding crisis we all viscerally experience today.

It brings together organizations, practitioners, and communities working across different fields to respond to the technical, ecological, economic, and cultural crises shaping our shared future.

Medulla — A physical space for turning shared urgency into collective resilience

About

Medulla is a physical space for turning shared urgency into collective resilience by building new collaborations and infrastructures of prosperity. Medulla takes its name from Medulla Oblongata, a part of the human brain and a critical command center for involuntary, life-sustaining functions. It brings together organizations, practitioners, and communities working across different fields to respond to the technical, ecological, economic, and cultural crises shaping our shared future.

Dresdener 113b

We believe change needs to happen in tangible places: in rooms, courtyards, buildings, and neighborhoods. Situated in a quiet Kreuzberg courtyard, Dresdener 113B serves as both a living room and a working space. The ground floor hosts Medulla's public programming; the floor above is dedicated to focused work.

Members

Medulla is made up of organizations and individuals working toward resilience in their own ways. In the space: nextlearning, Dark Matter Labs, One Resilient Earth, VVSSL, WissDem, CommunAid, and independent practitioners. We are assembling our first cohort of members and welcome applications.

Work with us

Work here with a flexible, dedicated or team desk or a day pass; hire the ground floor (120 m²), half the ground floor (46 m²) or the meeting room (16.5 m²); or host an event on evenings 18:00–21:30. See Spaces for details, or get in touch through the enquiry form at medulla.city/spaces or at hello@medulla.city. Dresdener Str. 113B, Berlin.
