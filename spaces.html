join the community

Thirty desks in a Kreuzberg courtyard; sixteen are open to members. Members meet monthly to shape the space together.

Make Medulla a regular base for your practice, with room for focused work, exchange across disciplines, and participation in the life of the space.

We welcome small organisations and independent practitioners whose work connects with our purpose and who are willing to share responsibility for the place.

Every member can host one event a month for free on the ground floor. Contributions are monthly and per person, set by how you join (personally, through a non-profit or through a for-profit organisation), not by the organisation's size, and can be discussed case by case. convene a gathering

Develop a workshop, conversation, or educational event within Medulla’s public programme.

We welcome proposals that engage with collective resilience, civic imagination, and the conditions for shared prosperity. Tell us which question deserves attention, whose perspectives are needed, and what the gathering could make possible.

  • Evening events generally take place between 18:00 and 21:30.
  • We're in a residential courtyard, so no loud music, and doors stay closed.
  • Each event has one named Event Lead, from setup to lock-up.
  • Shoes off inside, and the space is left as you found it.
  1. Tell us about your gathering: the question, who it's for, the date, and roughly how many people.
  2. We check the calendar and confirm with you.
  3. Once it's confirmed, you can start promoting it. We can list it on Medulla's events too.
