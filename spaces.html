host an event

Salons, lectures, workshops and gatherings on the ground floor.

Events at Medulla should connect to its purpose: turning shared urgency into collective resilience. If your work resonates with that, it belongs here.

  • The standard event window is 18:00–21:30.
  • We're in a residential courtyard, so no loud music, and doors stay closed.
  • Each event has one named Event Lead, from setup to lock-up.
  • Shoes off inside, and the space is left as you found it.
  1. Tell us about your event: what it is, the date, and roughly how many people.
  2. We check the calendar and confirm with you.
  3. Once it's confirmed, you can start promoting it. We can list it on Medulla's events too.
